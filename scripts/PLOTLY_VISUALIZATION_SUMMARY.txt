================================================================================
TECHNICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

Script: plot_size_vs_time_plotly.py
Algorithm: Piecewise Linear Regression with BIC
Segmentation: Exactly 3 segments per benchmark
Data source: Individual kernel timings (GPU-side) + benchmark output (CPU-side)
Aggregation: Mean and standard deviation across 8 MPI ranks
Iterations: 400 warmup + 400 timed per size

BIC Formula: BIC = n·log(RSS/n) + k·log(n)
- Lower BIC = Better model
- Balances fit quality vs. complexity

R² Formula: R² = 1 - (RSS/TSS)
- R² = 1.0: Perfect fit
- R² > 0.9: Excellent fit
- R² < 0.7: Noisy data (expected for small messages)
